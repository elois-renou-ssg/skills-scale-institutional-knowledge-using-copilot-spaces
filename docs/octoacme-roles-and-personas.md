# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads provide technical direction, coordinate implementation planning, and help the team make good trade-offs across architecture, delivery, and technical risk. They serve as the bridge between product intent and delivery execution.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architecture decisions
- Break down complex work into executable delivery plans
- Manage technical dependencies across teams and systems
- Support estimation and sequencing for roadmap milestones
- Help resolve design and implementation bottlenecks

### Goals
- Deliver stable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce ambiguity in engineering execution
- Improve predictability and quality of delivery

### Typical Communication
- Architecture discussions and technical design reviews
- Alignment with Product Managers and Project Managers on scope and sequencing
- Dependency and risk updates during planning and daily execution

### How they interact with other roles
- Work with Developers to clarify technical direction and implementation priorities
- Partner with Product Managers on feasibility, trade-offs, and timeline implications
- Coordinate with Project Managers on dependency tracking, risks, and milestone health

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads define the quality bar for the project, ensure the team verifies work against acceptance criteria, and help determine whether a release is ready to ship.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and validation approach
- Review acceptance criteria and identify coverage gaps
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Validate release readiness for milestones and production deployments
- Track defects, regressions, and test outcomes

### Goals
- Improve confidence in release quality
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Align quality checks with business and user expectations

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and QA status updates
- Review of defects, release readiness, and release notes
- Collaboration with Developers and Product Managers on acceptance criteria

### How they interact with other roles
- Work closely with Developers to verify functionality and resolve defects
- Partner with Product Managers to confirm user value and acceptance criteria are met
- Support Project Managers by flagging delivery blockers, risks, and release-readiness concerns

---

## Security & Compliance Reviewers

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Reviewers help protect the project from avoidable risk by validating security, privacy, and governance requirements throughout the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, data handling, and deployment practices for risk
- Validate compliance with security and privacy requirements
- Identify vulnerabilities, controls, and mitigation steps
- Support incident response planning and post-incident follow-up
- Ensure project decisions align with policy and regulatory expectations

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Build trust in delivery practices and release readiness
- Support safe and responsible product delivery

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, control checks, and risk assessments
- Escalation of high-risk findings or compliance gaps
- Coordination with Engineering Leads, PMs, and Stakeholders on mitigation plans

### How they interact with other roles
- Partner with Engineering Leads on secure design and mitigation planning
- Inform Project Managers and Product Managers of risk, timeline, or approval impacts
- Support Developers with secure implementation practices and validation requirements

---

## Executive Sponsor / Stakeholder Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Executive Sponsor or Stakeholder Sponsor provides business sponsorship, prioritization context, and decision support when trade-offs, budget, or strategic commitments need leadership input.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business priorities and sponsorship for the initiative
- Support cross-functional alignment and executive communication
- Help unblock dependencies that require leadership attention
- Approve changes with meaningful business or organizational impact

### Goals
- Maintain strategic alignment for the project
- Support high-value and time-sensitive decision making
- Ensure stakeholder expectations are understood and managed

### Typical Communication
- Steering updates, milestone reviews, and business status briefings
- Decision requests for scope changes, approvals, and escalations
- Strategy and risk discussions with Product and Project leaders

### How they interact with other roles
- Work with Product Managers on business impact and prioritization decisions
- Align with Project Managers to understand risks, status, and strategic trade-offs
- Partner with Engineering and Security leads when decisions affect technical or risk posture

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles help teams clarify accountability, improve cross-functional communication, and make project execution more consistent.
